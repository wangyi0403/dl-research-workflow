# Writing and evidence presentation

[English](WRITING_STANDARD_EN.md) · [中文详细规范](WRITING_STANDARD.md)

The article type, approved manuscript structure and verified venue instructions
take priority over template defaults. Record adopted structure and budget in 09,
venue requirements in 02, figure checks in 10 and manuscript verification in 11.

## Default research-article structure

| Section | Function | Initial English body-word range |
|---|---|---:|
| Introduction | Question, consequences, evidence gap and contribution | 600–1,000 |
| Related work | Recent work, comparison dimensions and differences | 500–800 |
| Methodology | Problem, assumptions, notation, mechanism and algorithm | 1,200–1,600 |
| Data and experimental setup | Data/splits, baselines, protocol and statistical design | 2,400–4,000 |
| Experimental results | Main comparisons, findings and uncertainty | 800–1,200 |
| In-depth analysis and discussion | Ablations, interpretation, robustness, efficiency and limits | 1,400–1,800 |
| Conclusion | Answer, insight, applications and follow-up tests | 200–350 |

These are planning ranges, not journal rules or quotas to fill. Adapt them to the
study and its venue; keep an approved existing structure. Do not add experiments
or figures solely to satisfy a word or display budget. Record counting definitions,
artifact version and justified deviations. Body counts exclude abstracts, captions,
tables, references, equations and other separately budgeted material.

## Build the argument before polishing

State the intended scientific answer, comparison, conditions and decisive evidence.
Map supporting claims to figures, tables, sources and manuscript positions. A method,
dataset, benchmark, engineering insight or negative result needs an appropriate
argument; no article is required to invent a model or fixed number of contributions.

Synthesize related work by scientific problem, assumptions, data needs, capabilities
and evaluation protocols. Compare recent work fairly and include relevant conflicting
evidence. Search omissions do not establish novelty; publication years and citation
counts are not evidence-quality quotas.

Keep the title, abstract, introduction, principal results and conclusion aligned to
the same supported promise. Remove generic background, repeated definitions and
project chronology before removing necessary reasoning. Main text retains decisive
evidence and its interpreting conditions; supplementary material carries complete
reproduction detail and secondary diagnostics.

## Preserve the author's intended judgment

Calibrate scope, comparison, conditions and modality within the claim itself. Do not
first create an exaggerated assertion and then retract it with generic caveats.
Retain supported direct conclusions; do not mechanically weaken them into tentative
attempts. Preserve an appropriate uncertainty once rather than repeat it in every
sentence. Add a limitation when study-specific evidence makes it material.

Read edited paragraphs with their context. Removing words must not remove the
supported answer, bridge from evidence to interpretation, factual comparison,
qualification or citation. Fluent prose is not a substitute for scientific reasoning.
Internal audit questions and debugging history belong in project records unless
their factual outcomes affect interpretation or reproducibility.

## Quantitative and visual evidence

Compare data access, tuning, inference budget and hardware conditions. Explain
resource-mismatched or literature-copied values separately from direct experiments.
Ablations can support a component's role without identifying a mechanism. Distinguish
association, physical explanation, causal effects and transfer claims by their evidence.

Define the independent unit, sample size, repeated runs and the meaning of uncertainty.
Distinguish statistical significance, effect size, practical importance and equivalence.
Report failures and contradictory outcomes with their actual provenance.

Figures and tables are selected by their scientific function. Keep units, honest
axes, uncertainty, legends and readable typography; preserve input data and plotting
code. Inspect the final rendering at its intended manuscript size. A short caption
identifies the object/task; panel labels preserve correspondences, while detailed
scientific explanation belongs in nearby prose or venue-required notes.

## Editing defaults

- Titles identify the scientific object and question without unnecessary labels or abbreviations.
- Abstracts give precise names and explain the method rather than rely on a framework name.
- Use consistent article self-reference and terminology; venue guidance overrides defaults.
- Choose tense by completed actions, established facts and current interpretation.
- Conclusions include the research answer, useful insight and study-specific follow-up tests.
- Verify references, numbers, declarations and compilation against the actual final source.

The default draft route is Chinese review followed by English LaTeX; a project or
venue can choose another language or format. AI detector scores are not a writing
quality target, and a style reference does not supply the study's facts.
