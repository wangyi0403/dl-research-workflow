# Diagnostic Scores

The runtime supports four-dimension (1–6) and optional ScholarEval (1–10) output fields. These are diagnostic summaries of their inputs, not calibrated acceptance probabilities or proof of scientific quality.

Use `quality_rubrics.md` for evidence to inspect and finding severity. A script can screen formatting and consistency; novelty, significance, literature support and validity require source-grounded review. A score never overrides a verified blocker, skipped check or unknown. Do not turn a threshold or penalty formula into an editorial verdict.

Online literature checks are optional and require the authorized scope and a currently available tool. Record coverage and unresolved sources. The default workflow does not require online scoring or additional model calls.
