# Prose Review and Polish Handoff

Use this reference for the `polish` diagnostic handoff. Manuscript changes belong to the authorized writing workflow. Confirmed project and venue rules govern structure, language, captions and length.

## Review priorities

1. Check that each claim follows from cited evidence and that the paragraph has a clear purpose.
2. Identify ambiguous sentences, undefined terms, broken transitions and inconsistent notation.
3. Prefer plain, precise wording. Choose voice and sentence length for readability; do not impose a fixed word limit or replace familiar words with ornate synonyms.
4. State supported findings directly. Qualify uncertainty where the evidence requires it; preserve genuine limitations and negative results.
5. Apply the project's caption policy. Short captions may identify the figure and panels, with explanation in a note or the main text; full sentences are not universally required.

## Blockers and extraction

Pause prose changes to the affected claim only when a verified scientific or evidence blocker makes them premature. A parser's failure to detect a section is an extraction uncertainty: inspect the source or rendered manuscript before calling the section absent.

Optional logic/expression scores are diagnostic annotations. Explain the actual issue and its consequence; scores alone neither block editing nor establish readiness.

## Handoff

Provide the location, original wording, supported concern and minimum proposed correction. Preserve technical meaning, citations and evidence. New technical content or substantive claim changes need their own evidence and applicable author decision.

Read enough surrounding context to resolve references and maintain continuity. Split long sections at semantic boundaries only when useful; section length does not automatically require extra agents or model calls. Recheck affected claims after substantive repair.
