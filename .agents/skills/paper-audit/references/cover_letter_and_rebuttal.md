# Cover Letter and Response to Reviewers

Use for an explicitly requested cover letter, rebuttal or revision verification. Follow the current venue and author instructions. Keep review material within its authorized boundary; preparing text does not authorize external sending or submission.

## Cover letter

State the actual title and article type, the supported contribution and why it serves the target readers. Use confirmed manuscript facts and current venue scope. The length and requested elements follow the venue; do not duplicate the abstract or promise acceptance.

Originality, simultaneous submission, author approval, ethics, conflicts, funding and data/code availability are separate factual declarations. Include only statements confirmed by the responsible author. Mark a missing decision for author input outside the letter; never populate a conventional “all authors approved / no conflicts” paragraph from assumption.

## Point-by-point response

Preserve every actual reviewer/editor concern with a stable comment ID and its context. One comment may contain several issues; identify them without dropping or altering the request. Reuse `paper/review_notes.md` or the project's established response record.

| Comment ID and concern | Response/decision and evidence | Affected claim or artifact | Current manuscript location/version | Verification and unresolved item |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Separate implemented change, evidence-backed disagreement, clarification and pending work. An accepted point requires the actual repair and any affected validation before writing “we have revised.” A planned experiment remains planned. A reasoned disagreement addresses the scientific issue with evidence; do not add an unsupported limitation or future-work promise to placate a reviewer.

For conflicting requests, explain the scientific basis of the chosen resolution. Respect manuscript scope and real resource constraints without changing the author's goal. A false premise should be corrected professionally. A response may clarify text without adopting an incorrect scientific claim.

## Verify and deliver

Check coverage of all comments and the accuracy of quoted revisions against the authoritative files. Generate page/line locators from the current rendered version, or use a stable section/equation/figure identifier when pagination is not fixed. Reconcile response, manuscript, supplement, result artifacts and declarations. Reuse valid checks and rerun affected ones only.

Deliver the requested response text and any necessary pending author decisions. Record unresolved scientific issues separately. Sending, uploading and submitting follow the project's corresponding authorization.
