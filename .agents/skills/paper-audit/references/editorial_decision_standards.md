# Editorial Assessment

Apply `CONSOLIDATION_RULES.md` and the workflow in `SKILL.md`. An assessment follows verified evidence and completed review scope; it is not a vote, fixed score threshold or prediction of a journal's decision.

- Merge findings about the same underlying cause and preserve distinct consequences.
- Verify automated findings against source context. A script report is not automatically an established defect.
- Resolve disagreement by checking assumptions, original sources and artifacts. Keep supported minority findings; record unresolved uncertainty without automatically choosing the more critical view.
- Explain an overall verdict through decisive evidence, repair requirements and limits. Reviewer agreement or a numeric average alone cannot justify it.
- Preserve the responsible author's scientific and submission decisions. External submission requires its own authorization.
