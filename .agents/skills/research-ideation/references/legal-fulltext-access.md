# Legal full-text access

Use only lawful and user-authorized routes: open-access repositories, publisher pages available to the user, institutional access already authorized by the user, interlibrary loan, author manuscripts, or a PDF supplied by the user. Never bypass authentication, CAPTCHAs, robots restrictions, paywalls, or other access controls.

For unavailable full text, record the metadata and evidence gap, then continue with abstracts and independently verifiable sources.
