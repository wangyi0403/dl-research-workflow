# Pre-Writing Readiness Checklist

Use before drafting or materially restructuring the manuscript. The purpose is to expose unresolved decisions early, not to freeze choices that later evidence disproves.

## Claim and evidence

- [ ] The paper type, target reader, central question, and central claim are stated.
- [ ] Each headline claim has a maximum permissible wording and linked Evidence/Experiment IDs.
- [ ] Observation, interpretation, mechanism, causal, and transfer claims are distinguished.
- [ ] Unsupported or disputed claims are marked for author decision rather than silently softened or strengthened.

## Numerical truth sources

- [ ] Every reported value has a result artifact, metric definition, unit, split, uncertainty treatment, and experiment ID.
- [ ] Tables, figures, abstract, highlights, conclusion, and supplementary material use the same approved values.
- [ ] Protocol deviations, excluded runs, failed/null results, and conflicting calculations are visible.
- [ ] Comparisons with prior work come from the original source and use a fair protocol.

## Figures and tables

- [ ] Each figure/table has a narrative role, Claim ID, source data/code or documented manual source, and planned manuscript location.
- [ ] Scales, uncertainty, sample size, color/grayscale interpretation, annotations, and captions are honest.
- [ ] The venue's current format and AI-image policy have been checked.
- [ ] Missing or provisional visual evidence is explicitly marked; prose does not assume it is complete.

## Literature and citations

- [ ] Closest work and material contradictions have been identified.
- [ ] Bibliographic identity/version and claim support are separate checks.
- [ ] Discovery-only, metadata-only, unresolved, conflicted, and verified sources are distinguishable.
- [ ] Citation style and bibliography tooling follow the target venue.

## Writing and source decisions

- [ ] The approved paper plan defines each section's reader question, evidence, and transition.
- [ ] Voice and language follow the author, user, and venue rather than a universal first-person rule.
- [ ] Existing single-file or multi-file source structure will be preserved unless migration is justified.
- [ ] Terminology, notation, method-variant names, and a representative example are consistent.
- [ ] Placeholders have owners and a condition for resolution.

## Policy and submission constraints

- [ ] Current official venue scope, article type, length/format limits, template version, data/code policy, disclosure requirements, and figure rules are recorded.
- [ ] Author, affiliation, funding, ethics, conflict, data availability, and AI-use decisions have responsible owners.
- [ ] External upload, submission, DOI creation, or public release still requires explicit authorization.

## Ready state

Drafting may proceed for the scope supported by verified evidence; mark non-blocking unresolved items with owners and closure conditions. An assigned owner alone does not clear a material scientific gap. For initial drafting, Gate E may defer only checks of consistency with unwritten prose under the `CONDITIONAL` rule in `SKILL.md`; scientific validity, data provenance, reproducibility, and required figure/table checks must pass. Close deferred checks after drafting and before final delivery. If new evidence changes the claim, update the formal project artifacts first and then revise the manuscript; do not preserve a stale “locked” story.
