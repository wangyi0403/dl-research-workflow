# Abstract Writing Guide

Write the abstract from the completed scientific content and the verified paper plan. Its promises must agree with the title, introduction, results and conclusion. Follow the current venue's word limit and structured/unstructured format.

## Select the content

Identify the question or consequential problem, the specific gap, what was done, the strongest supported result and its meaning. Use the functions relevant to this article; a theory, benchmark, negative-result or review paper need not follow a method-improvement story.

- Explain the actual operation or insight. A framework name or list of modules cannot replace the method.
- State the comparison, evaluation scope and units needed to interpret a key result. Use only verified values; do not insert an impressive number merely to satisfy a pattern.
- Distinguish a proposed mechanism from an identified mechanism, and an aspiration from a demonstrated capability.
- Carry a material scope condition in the claim itself. Detailed limitations belong in the discussion unless their omission would mislead the abstract reader.
- Expand necessary unfamiliar abbreviations; omit implementation trivia and citations unless the venue requires them.

## Verify

Read the abstract independently: can the target reader recover the question, contribution, decisive evidence and conclusion? Compare every quantitative and scientific promise with its source in the completed body. Remove unsupported novelty, universality or causal language and automatic self-weakening. Revise when this check reveals a concrete problem; no fixed sentence count or revision quota applies.

Optional worked patterns are indexed in [abstract-examples.md](examples/abstract-examples.md). They supply structure, not this study's facts or required contribution count.
