# Introduction Writing Guide

Establish why the research question matters, what the closest work already establishes, the remaining gap, and the precise answer this paper can support. Preserve the approved article structure and current venue requirements.

## Build the argument

Recover the target reader's current knowledge and the central Claim ID. Trace the gap to verified prior work or project observations; do not create novelty by omitting the closest work or claiming that no solution exists because a search returned nothing.

Choose an opening that fits the reader: define an unfamiliar task, move from a broader setting to a particular unresolved question, or introduce a concrete failure or contradiction. Avoid generic claims about the importance of an entire field. Application examples need supplied evidence and a role in the argument, not a preset count.

Explain the specific obstacle and the insight or design decision that addresses it. Describe the concrete contribution sufficiently to make the idea intelligible. A simple operation should remain simple; incremental work should accurately identify its increment. Credit inherited methods and distinguish parallel work from direct predecessors.

Summarize decisive evidence and scope at the level appropriate to the introduction. Contribution statements describe what readers can now know, use or verify; they are not an inventory of implementation modules. A benchmark, measurement, negative finding or theorem can be a contribution without a new model.

## Order and compression

Organize paragraphs around the reader's questions, not project chronology. Discuss prior approaches by their relevant assumptions, objectives or capabilities. A historical lineage is useful when it explains a design choice; do not force repeated prior-method/limitation pairs.

After the body is complete, reconcile the introduction with the results actually obtained. Keep decisive contrary evidence and conditions needed to interpret the headline claim. Move supporting detail when the main argument remains complete; do not substitute promotional novelty for missing evidence.

## Check

- The central question and supported answer are recoverable.
- The closest comparison and the actual difference are precise.
- Paragraphs establish connected judgments, with evidence or reasoning where needed.
- Method names, conditions, contribution statements and numerical promises agree with the body.
- No generic caveat retracts an assertion that should have been calibrated directly.
