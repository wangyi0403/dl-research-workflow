The four overview illustrations were generated with AI and checked against the workflow. They explain the project; they are not experimental results or evidence of gate approval. Gate locations, conditional drafting and recheck rules remain in the workflow guide. Evidence-chain IDs are illustrative, not completed experiments.

四张概览插画由 AI 生图制作，并核对流程、标签和箭头。它们用于理解项目，不是实验结果或 Gate 通过证明。检查点位置、条件起草与重验规则以工作流为准；证据链 ID 是结构示例。

The existing `workflow.drawio.svg` and `directory-data-flow.drawio.svg` are editable technical diagrams. They are separate artifacts, not editable sources for the new AI illustrations. The original PNGs remain as detailed reference views. Quantitative research figures must come from recorded data and reproducible plotting code.

已有 draw.io SVG 保留为可编辑技术关系图，与新 AI 插画是不同产物。原 PNG 保留供详细关系参考。定量科研图必须来自有记录的数据与可复现代码。
